\# Business Recommendations



\## 1. Reduce High-Discount Sales



High discount levels are strongly associated with poor profitability.



The analysis shows that discounts of 45% to 80% generated negative total profit.



\### Recommendation

\- Reduce excessive discounting.

\- Set approval limits for discounts above 30%.

\- Focus discounts on products with healthy profit margins.

\- Monitor profit impact before launching promotions.



\---



\## 2. Focus on High-Performing Products



Some products generate significantly higher sales and profit than others.



\### Recommendation

\- Identify and promote consistently profitable products.

\- Maintain sufficient inventory for high-performing products.

\- Use product-level profitability when deciding which products to promote.



\---



\## 3. Improve Loss-Making Transactions



The analysis identified 1,808 loss-making transactions, representing approximately 18.67% of all transactions.



\### Recommendation

\- Investigate products and customers responsible for recurring losses.

\- Review pricing and discount policies.

\- Identify products that frequently generate negative profit.



\---



\## 4. Focus on Technology



Technology is the strongest category in terms of sales and profit.



\### Recommendation

\- Continue investing in high-performing Technology products.

\- Analyze the most profitable Technology sub-categories.

\- Maintain inventory availability for high-demand products.



\---



\## 5. Improve Regional Performance



The West region generates the highest sales among the regions analyzed.



\### Recommendation

\- Study successful strategies used in the West.

\- Investigate lower-performing regions.

\- Develop region-specific sales strategies.



\---



\## 6. Focus on High-Value Customers



Consumer customers contribute the largest share of sales and profit.



\### Recommendation

\- Develop loyalty strategies for high-value customers.

\- Identify repeat customers with strong profitability.

\- Create targeted promotions based on customer value.



\---



\## 7. Monitor Profitability Alongside Sales



High sales do not always result in high profit.



\### Recommendation

Management should evaluate:



\- Sales

\- Profit

\- Profit Margin

\- Discount

\- Quantity



together rather than relying only on sales.



\---



\## Conclusion



The analysis indicates that profitability can be improved by controlling excessive discounts, reducing loss-making transactions, focusing on profitable products, and developing targeted strategies for high-performing categories, regions, and customer segments.
